## Structural invariants

**Whole-pathway co-instantiation.** Every structure below exists and is active from the outset. The
cord is not a relay with an input side and an output side that could be staged: the **pain gate**
closes a loop that leaves lamina I, reaches the periaqueductal gray and the lateral parabrachial
nucleus, returns through the rostral ventromedial medulla, and lands back on lamina II **at the first
synapse of the incoming line** — so the descending arm regulates the same synapse the ascending arm
starts from. The **gamma loop** closes entirely inside one segment (gamma motor neuron → intrafusal
spindle fibre → Ia afferent → alpha motor neuron), and the **Renshaw** loop closes on a motor neuron's
own collateral. A staged bring-up would leave all three open, and an open loop is not a loop.

**Per-edge latency.** Every edge carries a nonzero, finite, heterogeneous latency in real time units.
**This section is unusual in the set: three latencies are measured, in milliseconds, and the rest read
*to derive*.** The lamina-I spinothalamic edge carries three labelled lines with **central conduction
latencies of roughly 131, 59 and 72 ms** — nociceptive-specific, thermoreceptive-specific and
polymodal-nociceptive respectively. The spread is the point: **three lines on one anatomical edge, and
the slowest is 2.2× the fastest**, so an edge is not one delay even when it is one projection.

**The locomotor central pattern generator is the strongest case in the map against a global clock, and
it is a positive statement rather than a caution.** Its output *is* a rhythm: an intrinsic interneuron
network that generates the alternating flexor / extensor and left / right locomotor pattern **without
descending command and without sensory feedback**, from tonic drive alone. A network that manufactures
a temporal pattern out of a non-temporal input has a period, and that period is set by the delays and
time constants inside it. Break those with a uniform update step and the period becomes a property of
the step size — the walking rate would then be an artifact of the scheduler rather than a property of
the tissue. **Here the substitution of uniform delay for heterogeneous delay is directly observable in
the output**, which is not true of most structures in the map.

**One edge in this section is not a projection, and two point outside the map.** The rostral
ventrolateral medulla's presympathetic drive is **generated by intrinsically pacemaking cells rather
than relayed**, so its timing is a property of the source cells and not only of the wire. And the
motor-neuron output to **skeletal muscle** and the intermediolateral cell column's output to the
**sympathetic chain ganglia** both terminate on the body side of the scope boundary — declared as
such, exactly as the source declares them.

## Spinal cord grey matter

- **Function → structure.** The segmental processing core, laminated dorsal to ventral. The structure
  is the **Rexed lamina stack, I–X**, and the three functional divisions are laminar ranges within it.
  **Dorsal horn, laminae I–VI, sensory:** lamina I (marginal zone) is the origin of the lamina-I
  ascending projection carrying pain, temperature, itch and interoception; lamina II (substantia
  gelatinosa) is the **pain gate**, where descending control modulates incoming nociception **at the
  first synapse**; laminae III–VI (nucleus proprius and deeper) take mechanoreceptive and convergent
  input. **Intermediate zone, lamina VII:** three named cell groups inside one lamina — **Clarke's
  column / nucleus dorsalis** (≈ C8–L2/3), origin of the dorsal spinocerebellar tract; the
  **intermediolateral cell column** (≈ T1–L2/3), holding the sympathetic preganglionic neurons; and
  the **sacral parasympathetic nucleus** (S2–S4). **Ventral horn, laminae VIII–IX, motor:** the
  motor-neuron pools — **alpha** motor neurons to extrafusal muscle and **gamma** motor neurons to
  muscle-spindle intrafusal fibres — the final common path onto skeletal muscle. The pools are
  **somatotopic**: medial for axial and trunk, lateral for distal limb, with flexor pools dorsal and
  extensor pools ventral. **Lamina X** rings the central canal.

  **The dorsal horn is overwhelmingly local.** Projection neurons are a small minority: concentrated
  in lamina I, largely **absent from lamina II**, scattered through laminae III–VI. **90–95% of lamina
  I neurons and virtually all lamina II neurons are interneurons**, and about **80%** of the lamina I
  projection cells express the neurokinin-1 receptor. The interneurons divide into inhibitory
  (gamma-aminobutyric acid and/or glycine) and excitatory (glutamatergic) classes, many with
  propriospinal axons reaching several segments.

## Spinal cord white matter

- **Function → structure.** The cord's cabling. **Transmission, not processing** — it computes
  nothing, and it is inseparable from the cord, which is why it is a structure here rather than a
  tract in the white-matter file. Its sub-elements are the **three funiculi and the named long tracts
  within them**, and they are **parallel channels rather than a circuit**.
  **Ascending:** the **dorsal columns** — fasciculus **gracilis** (lower body, below ~T6) and
  fasciculus **cuneatus** (upper body, above ~T6) — fine touch, vibration and conscious
  proprioception, ascending **ipsilaterally**; the **anterolateral / spinothalamic** system — pain,
  temperature, crude touch, interoception — which **crosses at the segmental level** through the
  anterior white commissure and ascends **contralaterally**; and the **spinocerebellar** tracts,
  dorsal (from Clarke's column, ipsilateral, via the inferior peduncle) and ventral (**crossed, then
  re-crossing**, via the superior peduncle).
  **Descending:** the **lateral corticospinal** tract, **crossed at the medullary pyramidal
  decussation** (~85–90% of fibres), driving distal-limb voluntary movement, with the smaller
  **anterior corticospinal** tract uncrossed and axial; **reticulospinal** (posture, locomotion,
  tone); **vestibulospinal** (antigravity, balance); **rubrospinal** (small, largely vestigial in
  humans); **tectospinal** (head orienting); and the **descending modulatory** fibres — raphe and
  rostral ventromedial medulla to the dorsal horn, hypothalamospinal to the autonomic columns.

  **Crossings are load-bearing here and are stated per tract, not per entry.** Three distinct
  patterns coexist in one compartment: ipsilateral ascent (dorsal columns), crossing at the segment of
  entry (anterolateral), crossing far rostral of it (corticospinal, at the medulla), and a **double
  crossing** (ventral spinocerebellar, crossed then re-crossed). The double crossing is the one that
  cannot be re-derived from either endpoint, because both ends see a same-side connection.

- **Inputs / Outputs.** **Routing statements, not payload transfers.** No synapse occurs here, so

  here and no number is. The three measured lamina-I latencies are the only figures on the other side
  of that gap, and they are conduction *plus* synaptic time over an unstated path length rather than a
  per-tract conduction velocity.

- **Invariant conformance.** Co-instantiation: present from the outset; a cabling compartment cannot
  be deferred without disconnecting every edge in the entry above and every descending edge into it.
  Per-edge latency: **nonzero — the fibres have real length, and this is the one structure whose whole
  contribution to the system is delay** — while the edges themselves are routing statements and carry
  no synaptic term.

- **Justification (biological).** The compartment's organisation is the **inverse of the cerebrum**:
  central grey wrapped in peripheral white, rather than a grey sheet over a white core. That is a
  structural claim with a consequence for shape — there is no tangential sheet to tile here, and the
  white matter's extent is set by how much traffic passes a level rather than by a surface area. It is
  largest in the cervical cord, where the full ascending and descending traffic is present; no
  verified per-level figure is recorded, so that gradient is stated and not quantified.

## Spinal reflex arcs and central pattern generators

*(This entry is a **circuit, not a structure**: it adds no tissue, and its neurons are the grey-matter
entry's, already counted. It gets the full field set anyway, because the template exists so that a
missing field is visible at a glance, and because its edges and its timing are real even though its
sizing is zero. **That this belongs at this rung as an entry rather than as a note on the grey-matter
entry is Claude's reading**, taken from the source's own decision to give it a slot set of its own.)*

- **Function → structure.** The cord's **own computation**. Six reflex arcs and one pattern generator,
  all of them circuits among interneurons and motor neurons already counted above.
  **Reflex arcs:** the **monosynaptic stretch (myotatic) reflex** — Ia muscle-spindle afferent
  straight onto the alpha motor neuron, **the only monosynaptic reflex in the map**; **reciprocal
  inhibition**, a Ia inhibitory interneuron relaxing the antagonist; **Golgi-tendon (Ib) autogenic
  inhibition**; the polysynaptic **flexor-withdrawal reflex with crossed extension** — withdraw the
  stimulated limb, extend the other to bear weight; **Renshaw recurrent inhibition** of motor neurons;
  and the **gamma loop**, in which gamma motor neurons set spindle sensitivity under alpha–gamma
  co-activation.
  **Central pattern generators:** intrinsic **lumbar** (leg) and **cervical** (arm) interneuron
  networks generating the rhythmic, alternating flexor / extensor and left / right locomotor pattern
  **without descending command and without sensory feedback.** The brainstem **mesencephalic
  locomotor region** initiates stepping and sets its speed; the spinal network shapes the pattern.
  Human evidence includes rhythmic stepping-like output evoked by epidural stimulation of the lumbar
  cord after spinal-cord injury.

- **Inputs.** One, and only one, crosses a structure boundary.
  - **← mesencephalic locomotor region** (reticular formation) — a **start / speed command for
    locomotion**: not the pattern itself but the go-signal and its rate. The pattern generator shapes
    the rhythm; this input triggers and scales it. Fan-in: to derive. **Far-end dependency: source

    locomotion.

    start/speed command for locomotion named on it.** The word *locomotion* appears in that entry's
    Function field, alongside the breathing pattern generators, **and on no edge.** **The mesencephalic
    locomotor region is not named as a sub-element there either.** **So the start/speed payload this
    line depends on is typed here and at neither end of the producer's own declaration** — the
    producer has an edge to the cord and does not say this rides on it. **Owed at the source: type the
    locomotor limb at the producer, or establish that it is a separate edge from the postural one.**
    **Not written into the far file**, and this line is unchanged: it is the consumer's reading of a

  - All other afferents are the grey-matter entry's and are not re-declared.

- **Outputs.** **None of its own.** The efferent path is the grey-matter entry's motor-neuron output
  to skeletal muscle. Declaring it twice would put a transfer in the architecture that does not exist.

- **Internal edges.** Seven, all among the grey-matter entry's neurons. *(This is the field the
  template does not have — the architecture design records the internal-structure gap as open and
  Micky's to name. The edges are written here rather than dropped, under the field name the source

  - **Ia muscle-spindle afferent → alpha motor neuron**, monosynaptic — the stretch-reflex drive:

    reflex movement is not opposed. Fan-in / fan-out: to derive.
  - **Ib Golgi-tendon afferent → interneuron → homonymous alpha motor neuron** — autogenic
    inhibition, a tension signal limiting force. Fan-in / fan-out: to derive.
  - **nociceptive afferent → flexor interneuron pool → ipsilateral flexors and crossed extensors** —
    the polysynaptic withdrawal-with-crossed-extension pattern. **This edge crosses the midline
    inside the segment**, and the crossing is the function: one limb withdraws, the other loads.
    Fan-in / fan-out: to derive.
  - **alpha motor-neuron collateral → Renshaw cell → the same and neighbouring motor neurons** —
    recurrent inhibition, a negative-feedback gain control on motor-neuron output. Fan-in / fan-out:
    to derive.
  - **gamma motor neuron → intrafusal spindle fibre → Ia afferent → alpha motor neuron** — the gamma
    loop, setting spindle sensitivity so the stretch reflex stays responsive across muscle lengths.
    **Part of this loop's path is in muscle**, outside the scope boundary, so it is a loop the map
    can name and cannot close inside itself. Fan-in / fan-out: to derive.
  - **central-pattern-generator interneuron network → flexor / extensor and left / right motor
    pools** — the rhythmic alternating locomotor pattern, generated intrinsically. Fan-in / fan-out:
    to derive.

- **Sizing.** **None — no new tissue.** The interneurons and motor neurons are the grey-matter
  entry's, and their thresholds are counted there. **This entry's figure must not be added to the
  section total**, and the total below excludes it explicitly for that reason.

- **Shape.** **Not an array of its own.** Its state lives in the grey-matter entry's array. What it
  *does* require, and what nothing in this file supplies, is **connectivity within that array**: which
  Ia line reaches which alpha motor neuron, which Renshaw cell serves which pool, which interneurons
  belong to one pattern-generator network. **A fan-in count is not a connectivity pattern**, and no
  fan-in is even counted here — all seven internal edges read *to derive*.

- **Edge latency.** **To derive** throughout, and the ordering is stated even though the numbers are
  not: the monosynaptic stretch reflex is the **shortest loop in the cord** — one synapse, no
  interneuron — and the polysynaptic withdrawal reflex is longer by the interneuron pool it traverses.
  **The pattern generator's period is a latency question and not a rate question**, and it is the one
  number in this file whose absence changes what the system computes rather than only how large it is.

- **Invariant conformance.** Co-instantiation: the gamma loop and the Renshaw loop are closed inside a
  single segment and cannot be staged at all; the pattern generator is a loop with no external arm, so
  there is nothing to bring up around it. Per-edge latency: satisfied in form and **empty in content**
  — every internal edge reads to derive, and this is the entry where that matters most, because the
  delays *are* the rhythm.

- **Justification (biological).** A pattern generator that runs **without descending command and
  without sensory feedback** establishes that a low rung of the map builds a temporal pattern out of a
  tonic input. That is a claim about where timing is manufactured in the system: not only at the top,
  and not only by the periodicity of the input. Everything above it inherits a moving substrate.
